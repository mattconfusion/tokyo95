Tokyo95 Help Guide

Game Rules: Tokyo / Meyer

Tokyo is a classic dice bluffing game played with two dice. The objective is to be the last player with lives remaining.

  • Rolling: On your turn, roll two dice secretly.
  • Announcing: Announce a value. It must be higher than the previous player's claim. You can tell the truth or bluff.
  • Ranking:
    • 21 (Tokyo): The highest possible roll.
    • Pairs: 66 > 55 > 44 > 33 > 22 > 11.
    • Numbers: Higher combinations first (e.g., 65 > 64 > 54 > ... > 31).
  • Challenging: Instead of rolling, you can challenge the previous player's claim.
    • If they were bluffing, they lose 1 life.
    • If they were honest, you lose 1 life.
  • Tokyo Rule: If a player calls "Tokyo" (21), the next player MUST challenge.
